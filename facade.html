WHAT WE FIX

Every Facade Fails Differently.

Drag sideways — this is what our inspection is actually looking for.

01

Render & Stucco Cracking

Hairline to structural cracks from thermal movement and moisture ingress.

02

Efflorescence & Staining

White mineral bloom and water streaking that signals trapped moisture.

03

Spalling & Surface Loss

Flaking, crumbling render or brick face caused by freeze-thaw cycles.

04

Coating Failure

Chalking, peeling or discoloured paint no longer protecting the substrate.

05

Joint & Sealant Breakdown

Perished expansion joints letting water behind the facade system.

06

Glazing & Curtain Wall Seals

Failed gaskets around large glazing units causing draughts and leaks.

07

Balcony & Ledge Corrosion

Exposed rebar and rust staining where reinforcement meets the surface.

08

UV & Colour Fade

Sun-bleached facades losing pigment years before the substrate itself fails.

RESTORATION STAGES
01 / 04

Scaffold & Assess

Full access is rigged and every square metre is inspected up close — not just from the ground.

02 / 04

Strip & Prepare

Failed coatings, loose render and damaged material are removed back to a sound substrate.

03 / 04

Repair & Re-Render

Cracks are bridged, surfaces re-rendered and matched to the original texture and profile.

04 / 04

Seal & Coat

A weather-rated protective system is applied — built for the climate, not just the colour.

MATERIALS & SYSTEMS

Matched To What's Already On The Wall.

We don't run one system on every building — the substrate decides the approach.

Breathable systems for older masonry that needs to release moisture rather than trap it — the right call on most pre-war buildings.

Flexible topcoats that stretch across hairline cracks and stay sealed through seasonal movement.

Mortar matched in strength and colour to the original masonry, so repairs don't outlive — or outshine — the wall around them.

Protective barriers for concrete facades exposed to pollution and repeated wet-dry cycling.
