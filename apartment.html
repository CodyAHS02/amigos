ROOM BY ROOM

Every Room Gets Its Own Punch List.

Tap a room — the photo and checklist update to match what that space actually needs.

Kitchen

  • Degrease cabinets, countertops & splashback
  • Run and test every appliance
  • Recaulk sink and worktop joints
  • Tighten and replace cabinet hardware

Bathroom

  • Descale tiles, glass & fittings
  • Renew silicone seals around bath/shower
  • Check extractor fan & ventilation
  • Test taps, drains and water pressure

Bedroom

  • Patch and repaint walls & ceiling
  • Check wardrobe doors and runners
  • Test all outlets and light switches
  • Deep clean carpets or refinish flooring

Living Room

  • Full wall inspection and touch-up paint
  • Clean windows, sills and blinds
  • Check heating and any built-ins
  • Stage-ready final clean

Entryway

  • Test lock, buzzer and door closer
  • Touch-up scuffed skirting & frames
  • Confirm mailbox and key set
  • Final walkthrough photo log
