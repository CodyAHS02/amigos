WHAT'S ACTUALLY IN A WALL

Five Layers. One Flat Surface.

Click through the cross-section — this is what's behind every wall we close up.

01 Framing +

Studs set plumb, level and correctly spaced — every layer after this depends on this one being right.

02 Insulation & Vapour Barrier +

Acoustic or thermal batts and vapour control installed before a single sheet goes up.

03 Board Hanging +

Sheets fixed with the correct fastener pattern, orientation and staggered seams for strength.

04 Taping & Compound +

Every joint taped and mudded in multiple feathered coats — this is what stops cracks reappearing.

05 Sand & Finish +

Sanded to the finish level you actually need, dust-free and primer-ready.
